How the scores work · adjust what matters

Scores are comparison tools, not ratings from guests. Walking times are estimates. Caveats stay visible even when an option scores well.

Location: festival vs departure

Odori score = max(0, 100 × (1 − walk minutes ÷ 60)). Pickup score = max(0, 100 × (1 − walk minutes ÷ 20)); a vehicle transfer scores 0. Location = weighted average of those two scores.

Use midpoints of the source’s walking ranges. Odori means the west end where specified, otherwise the source’s Odori estimate; it is not a uniform central-festival destination. Polaris has only a subway estimate and is left unscored. Winter ice and ski bags can make walks 1.5–2× longer.

Bed/bath convenience

Privacy = min(real-bed bedrooms, 5) ÷ 5. Shared-bed fit = (5 − twin couples − 0.5 × small-double couples) ÷ 5. Bath capacity = min(independent bathing rooms, 5) ÷ 5. Convenience = 100 × their weighted average. Small doubles receive half credit by choice, not a measured comfort fact. Weights normalize to 100%; all zero uses equal weights. Four/five baths get credit even if shared; the detail panel identifies private hotel bathrooms.
